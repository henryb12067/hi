# Brief: first weapon, a greatsword

From the game's owner:

> Generate me one greatsword in this style: Create an original weapon model for this Roblox
> game, using a stylized low-poly dark-fantasy aesthetic inspired by the general feel of
> Deepwoken's equipment. Focus on strong silhouettes, angular shapes, restrained colors, and
> believable construction. Give the collection its own identity.

## What that means in practice

- **Original.** Take the *general feel* of Deepwoken's gear: grim, weathered, heavy,
  practical, a lived-in dark fantasy. Don't copy or closely echo any specific Deepwoken
  weapon's shape, name or colourway. This greatsword starts a collection with its own name,
  palette and shape language, which later weapons will follow.
- **Stylized low-poly.** Flat faces, hard angles, readable planes. No attempt at smooth curves.
  Detail comes from a few confident facets, not from many tiny parts.
- **Strong silhouette.** It should be recognisable as solid black on white at thumbnail size,
  and distinct from a generic cross-hilt longsword.
- **Angular shapes.** Chamfers, bevels, stepped facets, wedge-cut tips and guards.
- **Restrained colours.** A dark, desaturated base (iron, blackened steel, old leather, bone,
  ash) with at most one muted accent, used sparingly. No Neon glow unless it truly earns
  its place, and even then, tiny.
- **Believable construction.** It reads as forged and assembled: blade, ricasso or shoulders,
  guard, collar or ferrules, a wrapped grip long enough for two hands, and a pommel that
  could plausibly hold the tang. Weight should look like it sits where a smith would put it.
- **Scale.** A greatsword for a ~5.2-stud Roblox character: about 5.5 to 6.8 studs overall,
  with a grip of about 1.1 to 1.5 studs for two hands.
- **Practical on Roblox.** Built with native parts through `tools/weaponkit`. Aim for about
  40 to 110 parts, no z-fighting, nothing floating, and correct Tool handle and grip.
